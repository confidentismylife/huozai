# SafeStep原始资料申请清单与本地邮件草稿

日期：2026-10-14。用途：获取精确复现所需资料。以下是本地草稿，尚未发送；发件人姓名、单位和署名留待填写。

论文：Fang et al., *Development of a tenability-based path planning model for building fire evacuations using reinforcement learning*, Journal of Building Engineering 118 (2026), 115132. DOI：10.1016/j.jobe.2025.115132。

## 1. 优先申请最小资料包

先申请论文第4节的两个24格小案例，范围小于酒店案例，足以核对主要数值：

| 优先级 | 资料 | 需要说明 |
|---|---|---|
| 必需 | 每格随时间的FER或CO/HCN/CO2序列 | 时间单位、浓度单位、空间格编号、采样点位置与高度；覆盖起火至两种疏散完整区间 |
| 必需 | 网格与位置映射 | R1—R20、两个房间、两个出口到数据列/采样点的对应 |
| 必需 | 剂量计算约定 | V/D数值、CO2单位、秒/分钟转换、暴露采用移动前/后位置及积分规则 |
| 优先 | FDS输入和版本 | 案例1/2的几何、材料、3MW火曲线、门窗和通风条件、采样装置 |
| 优先 | DQN代码或训练细节 | 网络层、优化器、epsilon、种子、终止上限及目标网络更新周期 |
| 优先 | 非法动作与边界处理 | 训练动作、Bellman目标与推理是否各自屏蔽非法移动；撞墙是否耗时及暴露 |
| 优先 | 奖励计算次序 | 当前剂量先累加还是先取效用；出口步、FED≥1、截断终止的处理 |
| 可选 | 权重与具体输出路线 | 对齐报告FED=0.0441/0.1185及0.0695/0.5853，避免靠调参凑结果 |

若作者只能提供FER时序而不能提供完整FDS文件，也可以先核对轨迹积分。模型实现中已有FER/秒的CSV接口，但需先明确原始单位，另开原数据配置和目录，不能覆盖现有合成实验。

## 2. 建议的文件格式

可接受作者已有格式，无需要求其重新整理。若方便导出，FER可用宽表：

```text
time_s,R1,R2,...,R20,Exit_A,Exit_B,Room_A,Room_B
```

同时附单位和位置映射。若提供气体浓度而非FER，需要分别提供CO、HCN和CO2及其单位，不能仅凭列名自动猜测ppm、体积分数或百分比。

## 4. 英文邮件草稿

**Subject:** Request for data and implementation details to reproduce the two SafeStep benchmark cases

Dear Dr. Fang and Dr. Tam,

I am [name], a [position] at [institution], studying reinforcement learning for building-fire evacuation. I am preparing an independent reproduction of your paper, “Development of a tenability-based path planning model for building fire evacuations using reinforcement learning” (DOI: 10.1016/j.jobe.2025.115132).

I have reconstructed the 24-cell layout and implemented the published reward equations and DQN hyperparameters. My current tests use explicitly labelled synthetic hazard rates, so I do not regard them as a numerical reproduction of your FDS-based results.

Would you be willing to share the data for the two small benchmark cases in Section 4, particularly the per-cell FER or CO/HCN/CO2 time series, their units and location mapping, and the FED conversion parameters? The FDS input files and simulator version would also be very helpful, if available.

To align the learning and execution procedures, I would appreciate either the original code or clarification of the network architecture, optimizer, exploration schedule, random seeds, episode limits and reward-update order. One specific question is whether geometrically invalid moves are masked during action selection, TD-target calculation or final policy execution, and how time and exposure advance after an invalid move.

I would use the materials for academic reproduction, acknowledge and cite your work, and follow any applicable data-use conditions. A partial package, such as the FER time series and a short implementation note, would already be valuable.

Thank you for your time and for this work.

## 5. 收到资料后的核对顺序

先核对单位和24格映射，再对固定左右路线独立积分，检查论文终点FED；之后才对齐网络和训练过程。若剂量积分就不一致，应先查时间、位置与转换规则，而不是增加DQN训练量。
